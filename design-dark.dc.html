What the road taught me about ops

Pre-ride check

T-CLOCS before every ride. Health checks, smoke tests and a rollback plan before every deploy. Same discipline, different machine.

Smooth is fast

Jerky inputs get you into trouble in a corner. In delivery, small, frequent, boring releases beat the big bang every time.

Listen to the machine

You hear a tired bearing long before it fails. Good observability lets you hear your systems the same way.
